Bengaluru’s rapid vertical growth and complex water infrastructure—ranging from municipal Cauvery supply to deep borewells and tanker water—create unique hydraulic pressures and mineral scaling that standard plumbing simply cannot survive.

At Neerova, we engineered our standard of service around these local challenges. Every technician is trained in specialized diagnostics, heavy-duty material science, and clean non-invasive repairs that safeguard your home’s architectural integrity.
