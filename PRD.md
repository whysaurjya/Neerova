# Neerova Website PRD

## Project Overview

Neerova is a Bengaluru-based plumbing service company specializing in residential and commercial plumbing solutions. The website's primary objective is to generate qualified leads through quote requests, calls, and WhatsApp inquiries while establishing trust and credibility.

The website should communicate professionalism, reliability, transparency, and premium service quality.

---

# Business Goals

## Primary Goals

- Increase quote requests
- Increase phone inquiries
- Increase WhatsApp leads
- Establish trust with new visitors
- Showcase completed projects
- Improve local SEO visibility

## Secondary Goals

- Educate customers through blogs
- Build brand authority
- Improve customer confidence
- Highlight service coverage across Bengaluru
